How It Works

Our Signature
Process

Root. Reset. Rise.

Every Revana session follows a carefully designed sequence that takes you from surface tension all the way into deep subconscious transformation. Nothing is rushed. Every layer has a purpose.

I

Intention Setting and Breathwork

We open every session by setting a clear intention, an anchor for your transformation. Guided breathwork gently begins to regulate your nervous system, signal safety to your body, and open the energetic channels needed for deep release.

II

EFT Tapping

Emotional Freedom Technique tapping works on the body's meridian points to shift energetic blockages, clear emotional residue, and begin the process of rewiring. This creates the perfect bridge between the conscious and subconscious mind.

III

Somatic Yin Yoga

We move into 2 to 3 carefully chosen Yin poses, long held and deeply restorative postures that target the body's connective tissue and stored emotional patterns. Stillness becomes your superpower. We close in Savasana, where the real magic begins.

IV

Deep Hypnosis and Reprogramming

In the profound stillness of Savasana, when your mind is most open and receptive, we enter the hypnotic state. Guided visualization and positive suggestion gently overwrite limiting beliefs, planting new seeds of possibility, ease, and expansion.

V

Integration and Closing

We close by gently bringing your mind and body back to full awareness, allowing every shift to settle deeply into your being. You will walk away feeling lighter, renewed, and reconnected to your highest self.
