The Speaker

About Adeja

Healer. Visionary. Former Industry Powerhouse.

Before she was a speaker and healer, Adeja Allen spent over a decade behind the scenes of the music industry — booking global tours, negotiating six-figure deals, and operating at the highest levels of performance and pressure.

Behind the wins, she was running on empty. So she changed everything: she left the hustle, released 60 lbs of stored stress, traveled to 30+ countries, and immersed herself in hypnotherapy, Yin yoga, nervous-system science, and somatic healing.

Today she brings that rare blend of industry credibility and embodied wisdom to stages and studios — translating deep healing work into language that lands with executives, athletes, artists, and audiences of every kind.
